Privacy Policy for Blast Round

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Summary: Blast Round can be played as a standalone web game and through platform-specific builds, including an Android version distributed through app stores. The provider-neutral game stores gameplay progress, preferences, a local player profile, and local gameplay statistics on your device. The Android build may use Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner, rewarded, and interstitial advertising, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent and privacy choices where required. Other platform-specific builds may use the hosting platform's SDK for advertising, authorization, cloud saves, analytics, or other platform services. An optional Blast Round online leaderboard service may also be enabled in some builds.

2. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Blast Round, including the standalone web version, the Android application, and platform-specific builds distributed through game portals, app stores, social platforms, or similar services.

The platform through which you access Blast Round may independently collect or process information under its own privacy policy. Platform-level processing is controlled by that platform and is not governed solely by this Blast Round Privacy Policy.

3. Information stored locally by Blast Round

Blast Round stores information locally in your browser or device storage to provide game functionality. Depending on how you use the game, this may include:

  • game progress, level and boss progress,
  • scores, high scores, local leaderboards, and gameplay records,
  • coins, rewards, achievements, quests, milestones, and daily systems,
  • selected visual themes, difficulty, audio, haptic, and other settings,
  • tutorial and onboarding state,
  • a locally generated player identifier and a nickname you choose,
  • local gameplay statistics and run analytics used for in-game features.

In the provider-neutral game build, this information remains on your device and is not sent to the developer. Blast Round may use browser storage technologies such as localStorage or equivalent app or platform-provided storage to save this information.

4. Local gameplay analytics

Blast Round includes local gameplay analytics used to support features such as career statistics, progression, balancing, and recent run history. These records may include gameplay information such as score, cleared lines, combo values, run duration, selected mode or difficulty, power-up use, boss progress, and similar gameplay events.

In the provider-neutral game build, these gameplay analytics are stored locally and are not transmitted to an external analytics provider or to the developer. This local gameplay analytics feature is separate from technical and advertising-related data that may be processed by third-party SDKs such as Google Mobile Ads in an Android build.

5. Optional online leaderboard service

Blast Round contains an online leaderboard foundation that is disabled by default in the provider-neutral build. A platform-specific or future release may explicitly enable it.

If the online leaderboard is enabled, Blast Round may send the following information to the configured Blast Round leaderboard service:

  • a locally generated player identifier and an anonymous server-side player identifier,
  • the nickname you choose and your in-game career badge,
  • game build, mode, score, cleared lines, combo, boss and power-up metrics,
  • run duration and limited gameplay telemetry used to validate leaderboard submissions,
  • technical session identifiers and authentication tokens required to protect leaderboard integrity.

The leaderboard does not require your real name, email address, phone number, address, or social-media account. Do not use personal information as your in-game nickname if you do not want it to appear on a leaderboard.

6. Android application and Google Play

The Android version of Blast Round is packaged as a mobile application. It uses local app storage for game progress and settings and may use internet access for advertising, consent messages, privacy links, and other online platform functionality.

For normal gameplay, Blast Round does not require access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, messages, or similar sensitive device information. The Android build may use vibration or haptic features when available and enabled.

Information shown in Google Play's Data safety section is provided separately in Google Play Console and is intended to reflect the data practices of the Android app and its included SDKs.

7. Google AdMob and Google Mobile Ads SDK

The Android build of Blast Round may use Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising. Ad formats may include:

  • banner advertisements shown during gameplay,
  • rewarded advertisements that you may choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit, such as a daily reward or rescue feature,
  • interstitial advertisements displayed at natural transition points, such as after a game-over / play-again flow.

When Google Mobile Ads SDK is active, Google and participating advertising partners may automatically collect, receive, use, and share technical or advertising-related information for purposes such as ad delivery, ad measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and frequency control. Depending on the SDK configuration, device, region, and consent choices, this information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general or coarse location,
  • device and app identifiers, which may include Android advertising identifiers or app-scoped identifiers where available,
  • user interactions with the app or advertisements, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, clicks, and video views,
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as app or SDK startup performance, hangs, crashes, or related technical information,
  • advertising interaction and measurement information used to deliver, measure, limit, or protect advertising.

This advertising-related processing is performed by Google and, where applicable, Google's advertising partners under their own terms and privacy policies. Blast Round does not require you to provide your real name, email address, phone number, or similar identity information directly to the developer in order to view an advertisement or receive an in-game advertising reward.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use apps or sites that use Google services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Advertising personalization and consent management

Depending on your region, applicable law, and the choices you make, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, or otherwise subject to privacy-restricted treatment supported by Google.

Personalized advertising may use information about interests, activity, or identifiers to select ads that are more relevant to you, where permitted and where the required consent has been obtained. Non-personalized advertising is not based on past behavior for personalization, but it may still use contextual information and certain identifiers or storage technologies for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated reporting, fraud prevention, and security where permitted.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the Android build may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and a Google-certified consent message to request or record privacy choices where required. Depending on the message and your region, you may be offered options such as consenting, not consenting, or managing individual privacy choices.

Where required and supported, Blast Round may provide an in-app privacy option that allows you to reopen Google's privacy choices interface and update your advertising preferences. Changing your choice does not necessarily delete data that was lawfully processed before the change.

13. Data retention and deletion

Local Blast Round data remains on your device until it is reset or removed. Depending on the platform, you can remove local data by:

  • using Blast Round's in-game reset functionality,
  • clearing the site's storage or cookies in your browser,
  • clearing the application's storage through device settings, or
  • uninstalling the application where applicable.

If an online leaderboard build is enabled and you want to request deletion of data held by the Blast Round leaderboard service, contact gdoncorleone11@gmail.com. Please provide enough information to identify the relevant in-game profile or player identifier, but do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Advertising, consent, or platform data processed independently by Google or another third party is retained and managed according to that provider's own policies, legal obligations, and privacy controls. Such third-party data is not necessarily deleted by resetting local Blast Round game progress.
