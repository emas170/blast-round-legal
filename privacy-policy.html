Privacy Policy for Blast Round

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Summary: Blast Round currently works without user accounts, online leaderboards, multiplayer, third-party analytics, advertising networks, or a developer-operated backend. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device and are not sent to the developer.

1. Developer and contact

Blast Round is developed and published by Paweł Pierzchała.

Privacy contact: gdoncorleone11@gmail.com

2. Information collected by Blast Round

The current version of Blast Round does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or sensitive user data with the developer or with third parties.

The game does not currently require a user account and does not provide online multiplayer, cloud profiles, online leaderboards, or cloud-based save data.

3. Local game data

Blast Round stores certain information locally on your device in order to provide game functionality. Depending on how you use the game, this may include:

  • game progress and progression state,
  • scores and high scores,
  • coins, rewards, achievements, quests, and related gameplay state,
  • boss and level progress,
  • daily reward and tutorial state,
  • game settings and preferences,
  • local gameplay statistics used by the game.

This information remains on your device. It is not uploaded to a developer-operated server and is not used by the developer to identify you.

4. Analytics, advertising identifiers, and tracking

Blast Round does not currently use third-party analytics services to track users. Local gameplay statistics may be calculated and stored on the device for in-game functionality, but they are not transmitted to the developer or to an external analytics provider.

The current version of Blast Round does not intentionally generate or transmit advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, or similar identifiers for behavioural advertising or cross-service tracking.

5. Advertising

Blast Round does not currently use a third-party advertising network and does not transmit personal information or gameplay information to advertising providers.

If advertising or rewarded-ad services that process user or device data are introduced in a future release, this Privacy Policy and the application's Google Play Data Safety information will be updated to reflect the actual services and data practices before or alongside that release.

6. Device features and permissions

Blast Round does not use location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, messages, or similar personal or sensitive information as part of its current gameplay.

The final Android application package will be reviewed before release so that its requested permissions and Google Play disclosures remain consistent with the actual functionality of the application and with this Privacy Policy.

7. Third-party services and network connections

The current game build does not intentionally send gameplay or personal information to third-party analytics, advertising, or developer-operated backend services.

This Privacy Policy is hosted using GitHub Pages. If you choose to open the Privacy Policy link, your browser or device connects to GitHub's servers. GitHub may process ordinary technical connection information, such as an IP address, according to GitHub's own privacy practices. Blast Round and the developer do not receive that information from GitHub Pages.

8. Data retention and deletion

The developer does not maintain an external database containing Blast Round user accounts or personal gameplay data.

Locally stored game data remains on your device until you remove it. You can remove local data by:

  • using the reset functionality provided in the game,
  • clearing Blast Round's application data through Android settings, or
  • uninstalling the application.

Because the current version does not create user accounts or store personal gameplay data on developer-operated servers, there is no server-side Blast Round account data to request for deletion.

9. Security

Blast Round is currently designed so that gameplay progress and settings remain on the user's device instead of being stored on developer-operated servers. The developer does not receive those local records.

Protection of locally stored application data also depends on the security features of your device and operating system. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's privacy

Blast Round does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from other users. The current version does not require users to provide a name, email address, date of birth, location, or other personal information in order to play.

11. Future features

Future versions of Blast Round may introduce features such as online leaderboards, multiplayer, cloud services, analytics, advertising, or other network-based functionality.

If such a feature changes how user or device data is accessed, collected, used, retained, or shared, this Privacy Policy and the relevant Google Play disclosures will be updated to describe the actual data practices.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Blast Round's functionality, data practices, legal obligations, or third-party integrations change. The date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.
