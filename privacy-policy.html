Privacy Policy for Blast Round

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Summary: Blast Round can be played as a standalone web game and through third-party game platforms. The standalone/provider-neutral build stores gameplay progress, preferences, a local player profile, and local gameplay statistics on your device. Some platform-specific builds may use the hosting platform's SDK for advertising, authorization, cloud saves, platform analytics, or other platform services. An optional Blast Round online leaderboard service may also be enabled in some builds. This policy explains those cases below.

1. Developer and contact

Blast Round is developed and published by Paweł Pierzchała.

Privacy contact: gdoncorleone11@gmail.com

2. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Blast Round, including the standalone web version and platform-specific builds distributed through game portals, stores, social platforms, or similar services.

The platform through which you access Blast Round may independently collect or process information under its own privacy policy. Platform-level processing is controlled by that platform and is not governed solely by this Blast Round Privacy Policy.

3. Information stored locally by Blast Round

Blast Round stores information locally in your browser or device storage to provide game functionality. Depending on how you use the game, this may include:

  • game progress, level and boss progress,
  • scores, high scores, local leaderboards, and gameplay records,
  • coins, rewards, achievements, quests, milestones, and daily systems,
  • selected visual themes, difficulty, audio, haptic, and other settings,
  • tutorial and onboarding state,
  • a locally generated player identifier and a nickname you choose,
  • local gameplay statistics and run analytics used for in-game features.

In the provider-neutral standalone build, this information remains on your device and is not sent to the developer. Blast Round may use browser storage technologies such as localStorage or equivalent platform-provided storage to save this information.

4. Local analytics

Blast Round includes local gameplay analytics used to support features such as career statistics, progression, balancing, and recent run history. These records may include gameplay information such as score, cleared lines, combo values, run duration, selected mode or difficulty, power-up use, boss progress, and similar gameplay events.

In the provider-neutral standalone build, these analytics are stored locally and are not transmitted to an external analytics provider or to the developer.

5. Optional online leaderboard service

Blast Round contains an online leaderboard foundation that is disabled by default in the provider-neutral build. A platform-specific or future release may explicitly enable it.

If the online leaderboard is enabled, Blast Round may send the following information to the configured Blast Round leaderboard service:

  • a locally generated player identifier and an anonymous server-side player identifier,
  • the nickname you choose and your in-game career badge,
  • game build, mode, score, cleared lines, combo, boss and power-up metrics,
  • run duration and limited gameplay telemetry used to validate leaderboard submissions,
  • technical session identifiers and authentication tokens required to protect leaderboard integrity.

The leaderboard does not require your real name, email address, phone number, address, or social-media account. Do not use personal information as your in-game nickname if you do not want it to appear on a leaderboard.

6. Third-party game platforms and SDKs

Blast Round may be distributed through third-party services such as web-game portals, app platforms, social platforms, and game distribution networks. Depending on the version you play, a platform SDK may be used for functions such as:

  • game startup, pause/resume, and audio lifecycle handling,
  • advertising and rewarded advertising,
  • platform authorization or player identity,
  • cloud saves or platform storage,
  • platform achievements or leaderboards,
  • fraud prevention, security, performance measurement, or aggregated reporting.

Examples of platforms on which Blast Round may be distributed or submitted include GameDistribution, GamePix, CrazyGames, Y8, Playgama, itch.io, YouTube Playables, and similar services. Availability may vary over time.

These platforms may process technical information such as IP address, browser or device information, cookies or similar identifiers, advertising interactions, platform account information, and usage information according to their own privacy policies and consent mechanisms. The exact processing depends on the platform where you play.

7. Advertising

The provider-neutral standalone build does not include an independent third-party advertising network.

Some platform-specific builds of Blast Round may display interstitial, rewarded, or other advertising supplied by the platform or its advertising partners. In those cases, the platform or its advertising partners may process information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, frequency control, or—where permitted and consented to—personalization.

Blast Round does not require you to provide personal information directly to the developer in order to view or receive an in-game advertising reward.

8. YouTube Playables

If Blast Round is made available through YouTube Playables, the game will use YouTube-provided platform functionality and will follow the applicable YouTube Playables requirements. External advertising or monetization services will not be used inside the YouTube Playables build where prohibited by YouTube.

YouTube and Google may process information associated with your use of YouTube and Playables under their own privacy policies and account settings. Blast Round does not independently obtain your YouTube password or other Google account credentials.

9. Device features and permissions

Blast Round does not require access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, messages, or similar sensitive device information for normal gameplay.

The game may use standard browser or device features such as audio playback and vibration/haptic feedback when available and enabled.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

Blast Round itself primarily uses local browser/device storage for game state. A third-party hosting or distribution platform may use cookies, SDK storage, advertising identifiers, or similar technologies as described in that platform's privacy policy and consent interface.

11. Data retention and deletion

Local Blast Round data remains on your device until it is reset or removed. Depending on the platform, you can remove local data by:

  • using Blast Round's in-game reset functionality,
  • clearing the site's storage or cookies in your browser,
  • clearing the application's storage through device settings, or
  • uninstalling the application where applicable.

If an online leaderboard build is enabled and you want to request deletion of data held by the Blast Round leaderboard service, contact gdoncorleone11@gmail.com. Please provide enough information to identify the relevant in-game profile or player identifier, but do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Data processed independently by a third-party platform must be managed through that platform's privacy controls or support channels.

12. Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell Blast Round player personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party platforms when necessary to host, distribute, secure, monetize, or provide platform features for the game. Those services act under their own terms and privacy policies.

13. International processing

Third-party platforms used to distribute Blast Round may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy policies describe their international data-transfer practices. If the Blast Round online leaderboard is enabled, service providers used to operate that backend may also process data in the locations where their infrastructure is hosted.

14. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal data processed by the developer, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or to object to certain processing.

To exercise rights relating to data controlled directly by the Blast Round developer, contact gdoncorleone11@gmail.com. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

For information controlled by the platform through which you play, please use that platform's privacy tools or contact the platform directly.

15. Children's privacy

Blast Round is not designed to collect personal information from children. The game does not require a real name, email address, date of birth, precise location, or similar personal information to play.

Platform-specific age requirements may apply. In particular, a YouTube Playables version, if published, is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not designed specifically for children.

16. Security

Blast Round is designed to minimize direct collection of personal information. Local data is protected by the security features of your browser, device, and operating system. Where an online leaderboard is enabled, technical authentication and validation mechanisms are used to protect submissions and service integrity.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Blast Round's functionality, distribution channels, data practices, legal obligations, or third-party integrations change. The date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.
